African Child Kick Initiative
African Child Kick Initiative was created and launched amongst my peers and I to address the general unawareness about the situation of less fortrunate and vulnerable children within our respective countries in the East African region. This was a big issue for us considering the large amount of vulnerable children who need support from society as whole
Using repeat annual campaigns and a platform to create a network of children's home and shelters we aim to connect all these institutions to the general public. So far this has allowed us to provide for 65 children over the last two years and we are aiming to expand this over the next few years.
